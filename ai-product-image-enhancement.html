AI Product Image Enhancement FAQ

Common questions about professional product image enhancement.

What is AI product image enhancement?

AI product image enhancement improves aspects such as clarity, sharpness, lighting, color balance and overall image quality while keeping the product visually consistent.

Can you enhance low-quality product photos?

Yes. We can improve many low-quality product photographs, depending on the quality and details available in the original image.

Do you edit Amazon and marketplace images?

Yes. Product images can be prepared for Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Shopify and other eCommerce platforms.

Can you improve product colors and lighting?

Yes. Brightness, contrast, lighting and color balance can be adjusted to create a cleaner and more professional product presentation.

Will the product itself be changed?

The goal is to improve the image while keeping the product's important visual characteristics natural and consistent.

How can I send my product images?

You can contact DesignHaven through the website and share your product photographs for review and editing requirements.
